Privacy Policy

for the app “Crazy Rommé” and this website. The German version is the binding one.

In short
  • The app does not collect any personal data. There is no server, no account and no cloud.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • The app never connects to the internet. All opponents are computer players running on your device.
  • Only your current game, your statistics and your settings are stored – on your device.

2. What the app stores – and where

Crazy Rommé stores data only on your device, in an area only the app can access:

  • your current game (cards, table, score), so you can continue later,
  • your statistics: for each finished match the date, number of rounds, penalty points, winner and the levels of the computer opponents,
  • your settings: rules, number and level of opponents, card design, orientation, game speed and whether vibration is on.

This is not personal data. It never leaves your device and we never receive it.

3. Motion sensor and vibration

Two events in “Crazy mode” use the motion sensor: “Gravity” (tilting makes cards slide) and shaking (shuffles your hand). The sensor values are only processed inside the app during play and are neither stored nor shared. No permission is required and none is requested. Vibration uses the built-in Taptic Engine and can be turned off in the settings.

4. Permissions

The app does not ask for any permissions – no access to camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts or notifications.

5. Device backup

Apple’s automatic device backup (iCloud or computer) includes the app’s game, statistics and settings like any other app’s data, if you have backups turned on. Apple’s terms apply.

6. App Store

When you download the app, Apple processes data under its own privacy policy. If you agreed in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and statistics (e.g. number of installations). These cannot be traced back to you.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect it from abuse; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not use cookies, analytics or embedded third-party services.

8. Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and delete them once your request is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

9. Deleting data

You can delete your statistics in the app with the trash button on the “Statistik” screen. Deleting the app removes all its data from the device – but not from an existing backup.

10. Your rights

Since we receive no data about you through the app, there is nothing we could disclose, correct or delete. You still have your rights under the GDPR (including access, rectification, erasure and the right to complain to a supervisory authority). Questions: stille.werkstatt@tuta.com.

11. Changes

If data processing changes – for example through a later multiplayer feature over the network or Game Center – we will update this policy beforehand.
