Crazy Rommé

Rummy against up to five computer opponents – classic or completely crazy.

About the app

  • German-style Rommé (rummy) with 110 cards, for 2 to 6 players – you and 1 to 5 computer opponents
  • Five opponent levels from Easy to Cheater, almost every house rule configurable
  • Optional “Crazy mode” with random events and special cards
  • The app is in German. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no internet connection.
